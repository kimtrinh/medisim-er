The fastest way to report a problem with a case is Suggest an improvement in the debrief: it sends what happened in that case along with your note, so it can be fixed exactly.

Common questions

The team isn't talking. Check that your device's sound is on and not in silent mode, and that the sound button in MediSim is on.

My spoken order wasn't understood. Hold the mic button for the whole order and let go when you finish. Short, standard wording works best ("Epinephrine 1 milligram IV", "Charge to 200"). Every order also has a button. Microphone and speech-recognition permission can be changed in the device's Settings → MediSim ER.

The nurse said "Not yet". The team follows resuscitation timing: repeat shocks, epinephrine and pulse checks wait for the right point in the two-minute cycle, and the nurse tells you when it is due.

Is this a certification? No. MediSim ER is simulation practice. Scores, ranks and badges are not a credential and do not replace an accredited course.

Do I need an account? No. The app needs no sign-in, and your cases stay on your device. See the privacy policy for exactly what is and isn't stored.

Deleting your data. In the app, deleting the app removes everything it kept. On the website, clearing the site's data in your browser does the same; if you signed in, write to the address above and your account and training log will be deleted.

Requirements

The app runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, and on Mac with macOS 26 or later. Spoken orders work on every supported device; the extra help with unclear orders uses Apple Intelligence where the device supports it. The website runs in any current browser.
